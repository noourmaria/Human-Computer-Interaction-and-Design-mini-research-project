# ReadSeed: Supporting Reading Motivation in Children Aged 7-9

HCI mini research project (University of Greenwich, [Module name], 2026): design of a tablet reading app for children aged 7-9, with a Figma prototype and a proposed usability study.

> **Note:** the usability study was **proposed, not conducted**, so no user-testing results are reported.

## Design

| Feature | What it does | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|
| **Story Discovery** | Mood and genre filters narrow the library to a few story cards | Choice overload (Scheibehenne et al., 2010) |
| **Guided Reading** | Minimal reading view; word help and read-aloud only on demand | Cognitive load in digital storybooks (Takacs et al., 2015) |
| **Garden Reward** | A plant grows after a short reflection; no points or leaderboards | Rewards and intrinsic motivation (Deci et al., 1999) |

Gamified feedback is kept separate from reading so it adds no load while the child reads. Interface decisions follow Norman's principles (feedback, constraints, affordance, consistency).

## Proposed study

- **Participants:** 8-12 children aged 7-9, with guardian consent and child assent
- **Design:** within-subjects, three tasks, 15-20 minutes per session
- **Data:** observation sheet, simplified think-aloud, smiley-face questionnaire
- **Analysis:** descriptive statistics and thematic analysis
